About me

I'm an MCA graduate who started in software testing, working on both high and low-revenue casino and poker games. It taught me to sweat the details and to see how a product really performs.

Working with different teams on application and business performance, I became more interested in the data behind it. That curiosity led me to analytics. Today I'm building my skills in Excel, SQL, Power BI, Python and Tableau through hands-on projects, with the goal of turning raw data into insight that helps businesses decide better.

I'm looking to join a data-driven organization where I can apply these skills to real problems and keep growing. The projects below span healthcare, retail, food and supply chain.
