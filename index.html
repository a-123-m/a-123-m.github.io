About Me

I'm an MCA graduate who started my career in software testing, working on both high and low-revenue casino and poker games. That experience taught me to pay close attention to detail, think analytically, and understand how products perform in the real world.

As I worked with different teams and looked at application and business performance, I became increasingly interested in the data behind it. That curiosity led me to data analytics. Today I'm building my skills in Excel, SQL, Power BI, Python and Tableau through hands-on projects, with the goal of turning raw data into insights that help businesses make better decisions.

I'm looking to join a data-driven organization where I can apply these skills to real-world problems and keep growing. This portfolio showcases my projects across different sectors.
